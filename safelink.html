SafeLink — Live Demonstration

Reaching where the network can't.

SEPTEMBER 26, 2026 — NIGERIA

SafeLink is an offline BLE mesh emergency communication app, built to let people reach rescuers even when Wi-Fi, mobile networks, and internet connectivity are gone.

What to expect

  • Live demonstration of SafeLink's SOS and rescuer flow in the field, run device-to-device with no network connection.
  • Multi-hop relay showing messages passed between devices to extend range beyond a single Bluetooth connection.
  • Focus on real use cases — isolated roads, building collapses, and areas without mobile coverage in Nigeria.
